# Retail Sales Analysis Project

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail shopping behavior data to identify customer purchasing patterns, revenue drivers, and business opportunities. The analysis was performed using Python for EDA and Power BI for dashboard creation.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Power BI
- GitHub

## Dataset Features
- Customer ID
- Gender
- Age
- Category
- Quantity
- Price
- Payment Method
- Shopping Mall
- Revenue
- Age Group
- Month

## Key Findings

### Revenue Contribution
- Clothing generated the highest revenue contribution (45.3%).
- Shoes and Technology were the second and third highest revenue-generating categories.

### Customer Insights
- Female customers generated more revenue than male customers across most categories.
- The 26–55 age group contributed the majority of sales.

### Shopping Mall Analysis
- Mall of Istanbul generated the highest total revenue.
- Kanyon was a close second in overall sales performance.

### Revenue Trends
- Revenue was highest at the beginning of the year and stabilized in later months.
- Clothing remained the top-performing category throughout the year.

### Correlation Analysis
- Revenue has a very strong positive correlation with price (0.96).
- Customer age showed almost no relationship with revenue.

## Business Recommendations
- Focus marketing efforts on Clothing, Shoes, and Technology products.
- Increase average order value through premium product offerings and upselling.
- Use age groups for customer segmentation rather than revenue prediction.
- Continue monitoring pricing strategies because price has the strongest impact on revenue.
